# Phase 4: bounded spellcasting engine

Phase 4's bounded spellcasting engine is implemented on branch `codex/phase4-magic-foundation`. This milestone is an executable magic slice, not the complete SRD spell catalog or every class feature.

The first increment adds a pure domain derivation for class spellcasting ability, spell attack bonus, spell save DC, shared slot maxima, and separate Warlock Pact Magic slot maxima. It follows SRD 5.2.1 multiclass rules: Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer and Wizard levels count in full; Paladin and Ranger levels count at half rounded up; Warlock Pact Magic stays separate. A spellcasting query is available at `GET /characters/{id}/spellcasting`, and the summary is also present on the derived sheet. It requires no database change because the result is derived from existing progression state.

The second increment stores shared and Pact Magic slot expenditure in the character's existing progression state. `POST /characters/{id}/spell-slots/spend` spends one slot with a revision check and an audit event. The sheet and spellcasting query now report remaining slots; Short Rest restores Pact Magic and Long Rest restores both pools. Existing progression JSON needs no migration: absent expenditure means no slots spent. Integration coverage checks both pools across a process restart and verifies the HTTP route and stale-write response.

The third increment adds a separately pinned `rules.db.SpellContent` row with an intentionally narrow SRD 5.2.1 starter catalog: Cure Wounds. A character may prepare that spell for an eligible starting class at creation. The prepared choice is persisted with progression state. An out-of-combat, self-targeted cast validates the choice, class, slot, conscious/action-capable caster, trained worn armor and caller-asserted verbal/somatic components before rolling. Healing (2d8 per slot level plus the class's casting modifier), slot expenditure and a `SpellCast` audit event are committed atomically. Warlock Pact Magic slots can cast the prepared spell on a multiclass character. The rules migration adds `SpellContent` without changing prior pinned character or combat content.

The fourth increment establishes an immutable spell-pack version separate from the SRD ruleset version. The original Cure Wounds data and hash remain in `SpellContent`; a new `SpellPacks` table contains pack 2 with Cure Wounds and Healing Word. New characters pin pack 2 in progression JSON, while older state with no pack version continues to resolve pack 1. The cast path selects the character's pack and reads each spell's healing dice. Healing Word uses a Bonus Action, 60-foot range and verbal component in the catalog, but this slice still permits only self-casting outside combat. Startup validates both pinned hashes and refuses drift. A migration/restart integration test exercises legacy state; the HTTP test exercises Healing Word's 2d4 per slot level.

The fifth increment permits replacements of already prepared spells at the class's allowed time. Long Rest accepts replacements for Cleric/Druid (any number) and Paladin/Ranger (one); level-up accepts one replacement only when gaining a Bard, Sorcerer, or Warlock level. The validator checks the owned class, source preparation, unique resulting choices, and target class list in the character's pinned pack. A replacement is committed with the rest or level-up and its audit event. Tests cover denied Bard rest replacement, accepted Bard level replacement, Cleric rest replacement through HTTP, and persistence across restart.

The sixth increment adds prepared-spell allowances from the SRD class tables for levels 1–20. The spellcasting summary reports each class's prepared count and maximum. Creation and class level-up validate additions against the pinned pack, that class's spell list, its own level's spell access, and its prepared-spell maximum. A multiclass character's higher shared slots do not grant higher-level preparation in a lower-level class. Level-up accepts `additionalPreparedSpellIds`; choices persist and appear in its audit event. Wizard additions wait for a spellbook model. Current packs contain only level 1 spells and at most two options per class, so a character may have fewer selections than the SRD maximum. Tests cover class limits, duplicate/wrong-list rejection, HTTP level-up, and restart persistence.

The seventh increment adds immutable pack 3 alongside unchanged packs 1 and 2. It includes Fire Bolt, Sacred Flame, Burning Hands, Blur and Circle of Death alongside the two healing spells. Class-specific cantrip limits and known choices, Wizard spellbook membership, Fiend Warlock's granted Burning Hands, Sorcerer Quickened/Subtle Spell with Sorcery Points, and Warlock's level 6 Mystic Arcanum choice/use now persist in progression. Existing characters can explicitly adopt pack 3 through a revision-checked, audited command; adoption preserves old choices and can add eligible known cantrips. The class spellcasting summary reports known cantrip count and maximum.

Encounter casting uses Magic actions and Bonus Actions, enforces one slot expenditure per turn, and resolves ranged spell attacks, saving throws, healing, cantrip scaling, half damage on a successful save, damage defenses, and multi-target area damage. A concentrating Blur effect has an encounter duration, affects weapon and spell attacks, and ends after a failed Constitution save on damage or incapacitation. Slot use, Sorcery Points, Mystic Arcanum expenditure, all target HP changes, active effects and audit events share one optimistic SQLite transaction. Integration tests cover restart persistence, two-target resolution, stale revisions, concentration and class-specific casting. The existing self-cast route remains outside combat; it cannot bypass turn resources during an encounter.

Source research: [official SRD 5.2.1 PDF](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf), multiclass spellcasting pp. 23–25; Bard p. 30, Cleric p. 35, Druid p. 40, Paladin p. 52, Ranger p. 57, Sorcerer p. 64, Warlock pp. 70–71, Wizard p. 76; spellcasting rules pp. 103–106; Concentration p. 178. `RULES_SOURCES.md` records this source boundary.

## Delivered boundary and follow-on work

The Phase 4 foundation criteria above now have executable paths: cantrips and version adoption; a spellbook and examples of feature-granted spells, Metamagic and Mystic Arcanum; encounter casting with persistent concentration; and multi-target atomic resolution. It is deliberately a **seven-spell** pack. The API rejects unsupported effects instead of silently approximating them.

1. Expand the SRD catalog with new immutable packs and effect-specific resolvers. Only Fire Bolt and Sacred Flame are current cantrip choices, so Bard, Druid and Warlock have cantrip allowances but no selectable cantrip in pack 3. Only Quickened and Subtle Metamagic, Fiend's Burning Hands grant, and level 6 Circle of Death Mystic Arcanum are executable. Higher Arcanum levels and other always-prepared/subclass spells await content and mechanics.
2. Finish material inventory and gold costs. Component availability, sight, cover, range, cone/sphere membership, and special senses are caller-adjudicated facts; the engine checks the facts it receives but has no map or held-object model. Circle of Death's 500 GP component is required by caller assertion, not deducted from inventory. The current area cast cannot prove that the caller included every creature in the area.
3. Add rituals, reaction spells, broader concentration/ongoing effects, class feature interactions, Sorcerer slot conversion and other Metamagic options, Wizard copying/rituals and Arcane Recovery, and more spell attack/save modifiers. A generic full-SRD magic engine is not implied by this milestone.

The next content increment should add one executable option for each caster class lacking a pack-3 cantrip, with a new pack version and targeted tests. That work is after this bounded Phase 4 foundation.

Current validation: `dotnet test DndEngine.slnx --no-restore` passes 147 domain, 10 application and 41 integration tests. Automated coverage verifies the implemented routes and persistence; it does not establish full SRD spell coverage, geometry or combat balance.
