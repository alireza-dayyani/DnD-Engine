# Magic API: Phase 4 bounded engine

`GET /campaigns/{id}` includes `gameSeconds` and a campaign `revision`. `POST /campaigns/{id}/time/advance` accepts `{"seconds":600,"expectedRevision":n}` to advance deterministic game time between unfinished encounters. The update and audit event are atomic. This clock is separate from UTC event timestamps; existing campaigns migrate at game second zero.

Once combat starts, the first successful non-ending command in each round advances the campaign clock by six seconds and emits `CombatRoundTimed`. Ending a turn that wraps to the next round charges the round being left; the next round is charged by its first successful command. Starting or immediately ending an encounter does not advance time. The encounter, round marker, campaign revision and clock, character changes and events commit together. `GET /campaigns/{id}` and the language-effect check reflect the new time immediately after the command succeeds.

All commands use camelCase JSON and string enum values. Read `GET /spells` for the current immutable spell pack or `GET /spells?packVersion=1` through `=6` for older packs. New characters pin pack 7; old characters retain their pin. Pack 7 corrects Poison Spray to use a Constitution saving throw while preserving pack 6 for existing characters. `POST /characters/{id}/spell-pack/adopt` accepts `{"packVersion":"7","expectedRevision":n,"knownCantrips":[{"classId":"bard","spellId":"vicious-mockery"}]}`. Adoption is explicit, checks all existing choices against the new pack, appends an audit event, and cannot run while the character is enrolled in an unfinished encounter.

Pack 4 retains pack 3's seven spells and adds Poison Spray, Eldritch Blast and Vicious Mockery. Pack 5 adds Hellish Rebuke; pack 6 adds Comprehend Languages; pack 7 corrects Poison Spray's resolution. The catalog is intentionally small. A selected spell must belong to the character's class list unless a modeled class feature grants it. Current feature grant: a level 3+ Fiend Warlock always has Burning Hands prepared without counting against chosen preparation. Unsupported spell effects cannot be cast.

`POST /characters/{id}/spells/comprehend-languages/cast` accepts `classId`, `ritual`, `pool`, `spellLevel`, `verbalAvailable`, `somaticAvailable`, `materialAvailable`, `expectedCharacterRevision` and `expectedCampaignRevision`. For a ritual, set `pool:null`, `spellLevel:1` and `uninterrupted:true`; the caller confirms the 10-minute-plus-Action casting was not interrupted. A Wizard may cast an unprepared copy from their modeled spellbook when `spellbookAvailable:true`; other casters must have it prepared. Normal casting uses an eligible slot and six game seconds. Ritual casting uses no slot and advances 606 game seconds. Both modes create a one-hour effect from completion. The character, slot when applicable, campaign clock, effect expiry and audit event save atomically. Neither mode runs during an unfinished encounter. The material assertion represents the spell's soot and salt, which are not separately inventoried.

`POST /characters/{id}/spells/comprehend-languages/check` accepts `{"medium":"Written","perceived":true,"touchingSurface":true}` (or `Heard`/`Signed`). It reports whether the active spell grants understanding of literal meaning at the campaign's current game second. Written language requires touching its surface; the caller supplies perception and touch facts. This endpoint evaluates the magical effect only; it does not translate content or model languages known without magic.

`POST /srd-characters` accepts `preparedSpellIds`, `knownCantripIds`, and for Wizards `wizardSpellbookIds`. Wizard preparation must be a subset of the book. The pack has fewer Wizard spells than the SRD starting book allowance, so a Wizard can begin with a smaller modeled book. `POST /characters/{id}/level-up` accepts `additionalPreparedSpellIds`, `additionalCantripIds`, `cantripReplacement`, `additionalWizardSpellbookIds`, `additionalMetamagicOptions`, and `mysticArcanumSpellId` at the applicable class level. A Wizard may add at most two spells to the book on an existing Wizard level gain, or up to six on first acquiring the class. A Wizard can replace prepared book spells on Long Rest. Sorcerers can choose QuickenedSpell, SubtleSpell, DistantSpell or HeightenedSpell as Metamagic options. Warlocks may choose Circle of Death as their level 11 Mystic Arcanum. Long Rest resets Sorcery Points, created slots, expended Mystic Arcanum uses and once-per-rest recovery features. The sheet shows known cantrips, spellbook, always-prepared grants, Metamagic options, points, Arcanum choices/uses and recovery usage.

Font of Magic has `POST /characters/{id}/spell-slots/convert-to-points` with `{"pool":"Shared","spellLevel":1,"expectedRevision":n}` and `POST /characters/{id}/spell-slots/create` with `{"spellLevel":1,"expectedRevision":n}`. Conversion spends one available shared or Pact Magic slot, subject to the Sorcery Point maximum. Creation spends the SRD point cost, requires the applicable Sorcerer level and produces a temporary shared slot (levels 1–5); created slots are spent before regular slots and vanish on Long Rest. `sharedSlots[].maximum` remains the regular maximum, so `current` may exceed it while created slots exist; `createdAvailable` shows the temporary part. These character routes are unavailable during an unfinished encounter. There, use the corresponding `/combat/{id}/spell-slots/convert-to-points` and `/combat/{id}/spell-slots/create` routes with `combatantId` and `expectedRevision`. Conversion needs no action; creation spends a Bonus Action. A Short Rest request may include `arcaneRecoverySlotLevels`, `sorceryPointsToRestore` or `memorizeSpell` as applicable. Arcane Recovery and Sorcerous Restoration each work once per Long Rest; Wizard level 5+ may replace one prepared book spell on Short Rest.

`POST /characters/{id}/spells/cast-self` is the existing out-of-combat self-healing route for Cure Wounds and Healing Word. It requires a prepared spell, slot, components and the latest character revision. It rejects characters enrolled in an unfinished encounter. `POST /characters/{id}/spell-slots/spend` remains an audited manual slot expenditure and does not cast a spell.

During an active encounter, cast through `POST /combat/{id}/spells/cast`. Supply the latest encounter `expectedRevision`, the acting combatant's ID, its casting `classId`, a known/prepared/granted `spellId`, `spellLevel`, `pool` (`Shared`, `PactMagic`, or `null` for cantrips and Mystic Arcanum), `targets`, and explicit component availability. A spell attack or save rolls inside the command. The result contains each target's roll, damage/healing and HP change. One commit covers the encounter, all character HP, progression expenditure and audit events.

```json
{
  "combatantId":"CASTER-COMBATANT-ID",
  "classId":"cleric",
  "spellId":"sacred-flame",
  "pool":null,
  "spellLevel":0,
  "targets":[{
    "combatantId":"TARGET-COMBATANT-ID",
    "distanceFeet":30,
    "casterCanSeeTarget":true,
    "targetCanSeeCaster":true,
    "cover":"Half"
  }],
  "verbalAvailable":true,
  "somaticAvailable":true,
  "materialAvailable":false,
  "expectedRevision":12
}
```

For Burning Hands, include each creature in the asserted 15-foot cone as a target with its distance from the caster. For Circle of Death, supply `areaCenterDistanceFeet` (at most 150, or 300 with Distant Spell) and each target's `distanceFromAreaCenterFeet` (at most 60). Pack 4 also requires an inventory item `black-pearl-powder-500gp`; add it through the inventory route before enrollment. It represents crushed black pearl powder worth at least 500 GP and is not consumed. The engine validates the provided numbers but has no map to discover omitted targets or verify positions. `ignoreBlur` asserts Blindsight/Truesight for an attacker where applicable. Quickened, Subtle, Distant or Heightened Spell may be requested as `metamagic` for a Sorcerer spell; Heightened also requires `metamagicTargetId` for one saving-throw target. Distant doubles a non-Self range or changes Touch to 30 feet. Quickened changes an Action cast to a Bonus Action. Subtle waives ordinary V/S and no-cost M components, but not a priced component. Only one spell slot can be spent to cast spells on a turn; a Quickened spell also prevents a later level 1+ cast that turn.

Eldritch Blast requires one target entry per beam: one at character levels 1–4, two at 5–10, three at 11–16, and four at 17+. The same combatant may appear more than once; each beam rolls an attack and damage separately. Vicious Mockery can target a creature the caster sees or hears and imposes disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn after a failed Wisdom save. The penalty is persisted and consumed by a weapon or spell attack.

For a weapon attack against a defender who has Hellish Rebuke prepared, `POST /combat/{id}/attack` may include `"reaction":{"pool":"PactMagic","spellLevel":1,"verbalAvailable":true,"somaticAvailable":true,"sourceCover":"None"}` and the current `expectedRevision` alongside the normal attack fields. A direct, single-target damaging spell may include the same `reaction` object in `POST /combat/{id}/spells/cast`. The defender must see the source within 60 feet, have an unused reaction and an eligible slot, and remain able to act after taking positive damage. The source makes a Dexterity save against 2d10 Fire damage (half on success), plus 1d10 for each higher slot level. `sourceCover` supplies any cover bonus to that save. A miss or zero applied damage spends no slot or reaction. The attack or spell response includes `reactionSpell` when the trigger fires, and all damage and resource changes commit together. Area spells and multi-target or multi-beam spell casts do not yet accept declared reactions. Hellish Rebuke cannot be requested through the ordinary turn casting route.

Blur is a concentrating self spell lasting up to ten rounds. Its effect is persisted in the encounter, causes attack disadvantage unless the attacker asserts a qualifying special sense, and ends on expiry, incapacity, or a failed Constitution save after damage. The engine records concentration checks and endings as campaign events. As with the combat API's weapon attacks, spatial and visibility facts come from the caller; there is no geometric or inventory adjudicator.
